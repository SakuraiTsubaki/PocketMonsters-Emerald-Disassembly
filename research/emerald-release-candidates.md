# Emerald release candidates

This Disassembly foundation pass is independent of the Emerald Decompilation project. Three local filenames collapse to two byte-distinct identities: Japanese `BPEJ` and English `BPEE`. The two English files have identical SHA-1 and SHA-256 values and are represented once, with both observed names retained as provenance.

Both identities passed all shared GBA header checks. They remain `candidate`; no identity is promoted from the Decompilation repository, and the unobserved German, French, Italian, and Spanish releases are not synthesized here. Independent corroboration or a matching reproducible build is required for `verified` status.

No ROM binary or byte extract is included.
