# Emerald release candidates

This Disassembly foundation pass is independent of the Emerald Decompilation project. Seven local filenames collapse to six byte-distinct retail identities: Japanese `BPEJ`, English `BPEE`, German `BPED`, French `BPEF`, Italian `BPEI`, and Spanish `BPES`. The two English files have identical SHA-1 and SHA-256 values and are represented once, with both observed names retained as provenance.

All six identities passed all shared GBA header checks. They remain `candidate`; no identity is promoted from the Decompilation repository. Independent corroboration or a matching reproducible build is required for `verified` status.

No ROM binary or byte extract is included.
